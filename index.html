Terms of Service

lacy-clip-bot is a single-operator tool, not a public product. It watches a livestream Logan follows, detects moments worth clipping, creates the clip through the streaming platform's own clip feature, and reformats it into a short vertical video. Once approved for TikTok's Content Posting API, it publishes finished clips to a TikTok account Logan personally operates.

What it isn't

  • Not offered to other users — there's no sign-up, no accounts, no multi-tenant service.
  • Not used to post anything other than clips Logan reviews as part of running the tool.
  • Not used to scrape, harvest, or redistribute anyone's data beyond the single stream it's pointed at.

Changes

This tool and its scope may change as it develops. Material changes to what it does or which accounts it posts to will be reflected here before they take effect.

Privacy Policy

What's accessed

  • Twitch — a stream's public live status and clip-creation API, authorized via OAuth under Logan's own Twitch account.
  • TikTok — the Content Posting API, authorized via OAuth under a TikTok account Logan controls, used only to publish the clips this tool creates.

What's stored

Access tokens and the video files themselves are stored locally on Logan's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to a third-party server, sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

Retention & deletion

Tokens persist until Logan revokes them from his Twitch or TikTok account settings. Clip files are kept locally and deleted at Logan's discretion.
